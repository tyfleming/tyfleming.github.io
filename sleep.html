Research / 02

Decoding sleep from brain activity.

Instead of asking a model for only one stage label, I study how short windows of fMRI activity can yield a probability for each of four states: Wake, N1, N2, and N3.

See the methodExplore probabilities
01 / Question

A state estimate with uncertainty.

Sleep changes continuously, while stage labels are discrete. A useful decoder should show its uncertainty, especially near transitions between stages.

The workflow below follows one causal fMRI window from measured signals to a four-number probability vector. Its numbered quantities describe the method.

02 / Method

From window to probabilities.

0130 frames · 62.4 s

A short window

Use the previous 30 fMRI frames (62.4 seconds) to make a prediction at the window’s endpoint.

02333 → 39

Cortical signals

Summarize parcel activity with network averages and within-network residual signals.

0339 × 39

Connectivity geometry

Estimate a stabilized positive-definite connectivity matrix, including causal context.

04tangent space

Tangent features

Project each matrix into a common feature space and retain compact signal.

054 stages

Stage probabilities

A shrinkage LDA decoder produces scores calibrated into Wake, N1, N2, and N3 probabilities.

062 branches

Complementary cycle branch

An L2-regularized logistic model summarizes directed interaction patterns; its probabilities can be blended with the main decoder.

03 / Equations

Inside the decoder.

Both branches read the same causal fMRI window. One represents connectivity as geometry; the other summarizes directed cycles. They meet as four-stage probabilities.

01

Causal window

Input: one subject’s fMRI sequence, 333 cortical parcels per frame, and a motion-validity mask. The training label is the sleep stage at the endpoint.

$$\begin{gathered} W_t=[x_{t-29},\ldots,x_t]\in\mathbb R^{30\times333} \\ y_t\in\{\mathrm{Wake},\mathrm{N1},\mathrm{N2},\mathrm{N3}\}\end{gathered}$$

With TR = 2.08 s, this is 62.4 s of data. Endpoints move by five frames (10.4 s). At least 24 of 30 frames must pass the motion criterion, framewise displacement < 0.2 mm. Labels supervise training and evaluation; they are not model inputs. Output: one eligible window.

02

Network signals

Input: the 333 parcel values at each frame, grouped into 13 cortical networks. Each network contributes its mean and two modes of the residual activity after subtracting that mean.

$$\begin{gathered} m_{g,t}=\frac{1}{|g|}\sum_{j\in g}x_{j,t},\qquad r_{g,t}=x_{g,t}-m_{g,t}\mathbf 1 \\ f_t=[m_{1:13,t},\;a_{1:13,t}^{(1:2)}]\in\mathbb R^{39}\end{gathered}$$

The residual directions and scaling come from training subjects only. Output: a 30 × 39 signal window.

03

Positive-definite connectivity

Input: the motion-valid rows of the 30 × 39 window, at least 24 rows. Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage stabilizes their covariance; variance normalization converts it to correlation.

$$\begin{gathered} \widehat\Sigma_t=(1-\lambda)S_t+\lambda\mu I \\ C_t=D_t^{-1/2}\widehat\Sigma_tD_t^{-1/2}\in\mathcal S_{++}^{39}\end{gathered}$$

\(S\) is sample covariance, \(\lambda\) the estimated shrinkage, \(\mu\) the mean variance, and \(D\) the diagonal of \(\widehat\Sigma\). The implementation symmetrizes, floors eigenvalues at \(10^{-6}\max(\lambda_{\max},1)\), and renormalizes the diagonal to one. Output: a symmetric 39 × 39 connectivity matrix.

04

Causal history in tangent space

Input: connectivity at the current endpoint and nominally 25 and 50 frames earlier (52 and 104 s). If an exact earlier window is ineligible, the latest eligible endpoint no later than the target lag is used. Each lag reference is a log-Euclidean mean fitted on training subjects.

$$\begin{gathered} G_\ell=\exp\!\left(\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i\in\mathrm{train}}\log C_{i,\ell}\right) \\ T_{t,\ell}=\log\!\left(G_\ell^{-1/2}C_{t,\ell}G_\ell^{-1/2}\right) \\ v_t=\left[\sqrt{\frac47}\operatorname{svec}(T_{t,0}),\;\sqrt{\frac27}\operatorname{svec}(T_{t,25}),\;\sqrt{\frac17}\operatorname{svec}(T_{t,50})\right]\in\mathbb R^{2340}\end{gathered}$$

The matrix logarithm acts on eigenvalues, not entries. \(\operatorname{svec}\) keeps 39 diagonal and 741 upper-triangle entries, multiplying off-diagonal entries by \(\sqrt 2\): 780 values per lag. Each of 2,340 features is clipped at training quantiles 0.005 and 0.995; training-fitted whitened PCA retains 64. Output: \(z_t\in\mathbb R^{64}\).

05

Tangent-stage decoder

Input: the 64 features. Shrinkage linear discriminant analysis compares the vector with four training-stage means under a shared covariance and equal class priors.

$$\begin{gathered} \delta_k(z)=z^\top\widetilde\Sigma^{-1}\mu_k-\frac12\mu_k^\top\widetilde\Sigma^{-1}\mu_k+\log\frac14 \\ p_{\mathrm{raw},k}=\frac{e^{\delta_k}}{\sum_j e^{\delta_j}},\qquad p_{\mathrm{tan},k}=\frac{p_{\mathrm{raw},k}^{1/T}}{\sum_jp_{\mathrm{raw},j}^{1/T}}\end{gathered}$$

\(\widetilde\Sigma\) is the automatically shrunk within-stage covariance. Temperature \(T\) is selected with participant-grouped training predictions. The discriminant equation summarizes the scikit-learn estimator; the probability temperature transform is literal. Output: four tangent-branch probabilities.

06

Directed-cycle decoder

Input: the same 30 × 333 window and a fixed, symmetric six-nearest-neighbor atlas graph (333 parcels, 1,152 edges, 986 triangles). At lags \(\tau\in\{1,2,3\}\) volumes, only pairs of motion-valid frames contribute to directed edge flow. The \(\widetilde x\) terms below are separately centered and unit-normalized early and late parcel series.

$$\begin{gathered} f_{ij}^{(\tau)}=\sum_u\widetilde x_{u,i}^{\mathrm{early}}\widetilde x_{u,j}^{\mathrm{late}}-\sum_u\widetilde x_{u,j}^{\mathrm{early}}\widetilde x_{u,i}^{\mathrm{late}} \\ f=B_1^\top\phi+B_2\psi+h\end{gathered}$$

The second equation is the conceptual graph Hodge decomposition: gradient, curl, and harmonic cycle flow. With harmonic basis \(H\), gradient basis \(Q_G\), and curl basis \(Q_C\), each lag yields five summaries:

$$E(f)=\left[\|f\|,\;\|H^\top f\|,\;\frac{\|H^\top f\|^2}{\|f\|^2},\;\frac{\|Q_G^\top f\|^2}{\|f\|^2},\;\frac{\|Q_C^\top f\|^2}{\|f\|^2}\right]$$

These give 15 lag-specific features. Five more come from the pooled flow \(\bar f=(4f^{(1)}+2f^{(2)}+f^{(3)})/7\). The final three measure change in the 75-dimensional harmonic coordinates between the current and previous eligible windows: \(\|\Delta h\|\), direction cosine, and its angle. The raw 75 coordinates are not direct classifier inputs.

$$p_{\mathrm{cycle},k}=\frac{\exp(\beta_k^\top e+b_k)}{\sum_j\exp(\beta_j^\top e+b_j)},\qquad e\in\mathbb R^{23}$$

Output: four probabilities from an L2-regularized multinomial logistic model, after training-fitted feature standardization. Supervised cycle fitting uses endpoints ten volumes apart; inference covers the full five-volume endpoint grid.

07

Blend and calibrate

Input: two four-stage probability vectors. A cycle weight \(\alpha\) is selected from \(\{0,.01,.02,.05,.10,.20,.30\}\) using predictions from training participants. A final temperature is fitted there after blending.

$$\begin{gathered} q_k=(1-\alpha)p_{\mathrm{tan},k}+\alpha p_{\mathrm{cycle},k} \\ p_k=\frac{q_k^{1/T_{\mathrm{final}}}}{\sum_jq_j^{1/T_{\mathrm{final}}}}\end{gathered}$$

Output: \(p=(p_{\mathrm{Wake}},p_{\mathrm{N1}},p_{\mathrm{N2}},p_{\mathrm{N3}})\), where every value is nonnegative and \(\sum_kp_k=1\). The largest probability is the predicted stage; the full vector shows uncertainty.

08

Simplex projection

Input: the final four probabilities. Each stage is one vertex of a regular tetrahedron. The point inside it is their probability-weighted average.

$$\begin{gathered} r=\sum_{k=1}^{4}p_kv_k \\ v_{\mathrm{Wake}}=(1,1,1),\qquad v_{\mathrm{N1}}=(1,-1,-1) \\ v_{\mathrm{N2}}=(-1,1,-1),\qquad v_{\mathrm{N3}}=(-1,-1,1)\end{gathered}$$

The view rotates and projects \(r\) to two dimensions. Rotation changes the viewpoint, never the probabilities. Output: the plotted point and a four-value text readout.

All fitted transforms exclude held-out subjects. In this exploratory implementation, blend-weight selection used calibrated tangent probabilities and uncalibrated cycle probabilities; held-out blending used calibrated outputs from both branches.
